Before You Ask

Things People Usually Ask

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on all unworn items with original tags attached. Simply contact our support team and we'll arrange a pickup. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery (2–3 business days) is available at ₹299. We ship free on orders over ₹5,000 (standard speed).

Do you offer custom tailoring or alterations?

Yes! We offer custom tailoring on select ethnic wear items. Contact us via WhatsApp with your measurements and requirements for a personalised quote.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also WhatsApp us your order ID for real-time updates.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within India only. International shipping to select countries is coming soon! Join our newsletter to be the first to know when it launches.
